DEADZONE COMMAND
OPERATION LIBERTY — NEW YORK CITY RECLAMATION RTS
MISSION OBJECTIVE: Retake New York City from the zombie infestation! Advance through avenues, intersections, and city blocks to destroy all 7 Mutated Hives and clear zombie forces across all 6 City Districts while defending your Battery Park HQ.

URBAN WARFARE & HIVE INTEL:
• 6 NYC Districts: Reclaim Battery Park LZ, Financial District, SoHo & Village, Lower East Side, Midtown & Times Sq, and Central Park North for Scrap & Fuel liberation bonuses.
• 4 Mutated Hive Classes: Subway Breach Hives (Runners), Spore Spire Hives (Acid Spitters), Flesh-Foundry Hives (Juggernauts), and the Apex Overmind Hive (Elite Waves).
• Urban Economy & Combined Arms: Claim city Supply Depots [R], deploy Battalions [N], load troops into Armored Vehicles [M] (UH-60 Black Hawks fly over skyscrapers & rubble), fortify streets with Walls [L] / Gates [G], and call Howitzer [Z] or Cruise Missile [I] strikes.
• Survivors & Hostile Mercenaries: Cyan Survivor Signals [J] mark estimated survivor zones (some are false leads). Right-click a Locked Shelter door with a ground unit to breach it, or right-click survivors at an Armed Outpost to evacuate them — then escort or transport them to your HQ, Barracks or Battalion HQ. Beware Bandit Territories: fortified mercenary gangs that fight both you and the infected.
• Infection & Medics: Zombie scratches can infect humans (☣) — they turn after a short delay. Train Combat Medics at the Barracks [M] to cure them (6 treatments, then a recharge). Rescued survivors join your command; bring them near HQ and use Return to HQ [R].
• Logistics & Armor: Structures take time to build (Engineers nearby speed it up). New Rocket Trucks, Flame APCs, Apache gunships & B-1B Bombers at the Motor Pool; Combat Mechanics [R] repair buildings & vehicles; abandoned civilian cars can be hotwired by infantry. Toggle ∞ RESOURCES (bottom-left) any time.
• Supply Logistics: Every weapon has limited ammunition, troops get hungry and vehicles burn fuel. Keep Supply Trucks [U] / Heavy [H] / Tracked [K] and the Supply Chinook near your forces — RESUPPLY [E], TRANSFER [F], and REFILL AT HQ [Q] (costs Scrap & Fuel).
• Air Power: Build an Airbase [P] at the HQ to produce aircraft and store up to 3 helicopters & 3 planes (stored aircraft refuel). Helicopters LAND / TAKE OFF [O] to load troops, or rappel them out while hovering. The infected now act as a hive mind — gunfire draws coordinated packs.
• Movement & Infinite Map: Soldiers and vehicles now have momentum — they build up speed, brake and turn like the real thing (tanks pivot, trucks need room to stop). Pick ∞ INFINITE MAP MODE on the City Map tab for an endless city that streams in around your forces.
• Controls: W A S D / Arrow Keys Move camera • X Stop • H Hold • U Unload • Mouse Wheel / +/- Zoom • Space Tactical Pause • F1–F4 Formations • [1]–[6] Squads • 💾 Save / Load.
